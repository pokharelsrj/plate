Plate — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Plate is a personal health and training log. It is a client for a server you choose: the app stores almost nothing itself and sends your data to whichever backend you point it at.

Who operates the server

This matters more than anything else on this page, because it decides who can see your data.

  • If you use the default server (api.srijanpokharel.com), it is operated privately by Srijan Pokharel and runs on hardware in a private residence. As its administrator, he has technical access to the database, including your entries.
  • If you run your own server — the backend is open source and self-hosting is the intended setup — your data goes only to your machine, and nobody else can read it. Change the address under Advanced on the sign-in screen.

Accounts are created by an administrator; there is no public sign-up.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

Plate reads steps, sleep analysis, resting heart rate and active energy. It never writes anything to Apple Health.

HealthKit data is used only to display your own history inside the app. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold or shared with data brokers, and never disclosed to third parties for their own purposes.

You grant this access explicitly in iOS and can revoke it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Plate. The app keeps working without it; the Apple Health figures simply stop updating.

How it is stored and protected

  • Your data lives in a SQLite database on the server you configured. Each account's records are separated by account, and one account cannot read another's.
  • Passwords are stored only as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, never in plain text.
  • Your API key is held in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Traffic to the default server uses HTTPS. If you point the app at a server on your own network, that connection may be plain HTTP — it never leaves your network.
  • A small cache of recent figures is shared with the widget and Apple Watch app through a private app group on your device.

What is not done

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs. The app talks to your server and to Apple Health, and nothing else.
  • No tracking, in the App Store sense or any other. Your data is never linked to you across other apps or websites.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for anyone else's purposes.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until it is deleted. Individual entries can be removed in the app. Deleting an account permanently deletes everything belonging to it — workouts, body measurements, Apple Health days, nutrition and check-ins — in the same operation.

To have your account and its data deleted from the default server, email the address below. If you run your own server, you control this directly.

Children

Plate is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not created for them.
